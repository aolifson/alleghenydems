ACDC Site Guide Overview Managing Content Local Committee Sites Costs

What It Costs

Two services power the site: Sanity (the content editor & database) and Vercel (the hosting). This page models costs on the assumption that most local committees will want to manage their own content — each self-managing committee adds one paid editor seat. Figures reflect 2026 pricing.

The Two Services

Service What it does What it costs (2026)
Vercel
Hosts the website		 Serves every page load, for the county site and every committee site — they all run on one deployment. Pro — $20/mo (per team member; one is enough). Includes $20 of usage, 1 TB fast data transfer, and 10M edge requests per month; overage $0.15/GB. The free Hobby plan technically fits our traffic (~9K views/mo) but its terms restrict it to personal use — an organization belongs on Pro.
Sanity
Content editor & database		 Stores and delivers all content (events, news, voter guide, tracker, roster…). Pages are cached, so Sanity is only queried when content changes — not on every visit. Growth — $15/editor/mo (~$12/seat billed annually). Needed for committee self-management, because scoped committee logins use custom editor roles, a paid feature. Every editor — county or committee — is a billed seat; read-only "viewer" accounts are free.
⚠️

Why not free? Sanity's free plan is generous (up to 20 users, 500K API requests/mo) and would cover a purely county-managed site. But it doesn't include custom roles — and custom roles are what stop a Fox Chapel editor from editing Mt. Lebanon's content. As soon as committees log in themselves, you need Growth.

The Monthly Bill

The formula is simple: $20 (hosting) + $15 × every editor seat — county editors plus one per self-managing committee.

Scenario (assumes 2 county editors)Sanity seatsMonthly total
County-managed only (no committee logins) 0 — free plan $20
3 self-managing committees 5 × $15 = $75 $95
5 self-managing committees 7 × $15 = $105 $125
10 self-managing committees 12 × $15 = $180 $200
20 self-managing committees 22 × $15 = $330 $350
💡

Two ways to trim the bill: annual billing drops seats to roughly $12/mo (5 committees ≈ $104/mo total), and a committee can share one login among its volunteers — one seat per committee, not per person. Committees that stay county-managed cost nothing.

Committee sites add no hosting cost

Every committee site runs on the same Vercel deployment. Adding Fox Chapel, Mt. Lebanon, or any other committee adds nothing to hosting — it's just another domain pointed at the same app. The only per-committee cost is the editor seat, and only if they self-manage.

📈

Election-season spikes are covered. Even 5× normal traffic (~45K views/mo, tens of GB) stays far under Vercel Pro's 1 TB — no overage charges expected. Costs scale with how many committees want logins, not with traffic.

Pricing verified July 2026 against Vercel's and Sanity's published plans. Plan terms change — confirm current pricing before committing, especially whether custom editor roles sit on Sanity's Growth vs. Enterprise tier for your account.
