The Features at a Glance

A short tour. Each feature links to its detailed guide.

🛠️ One Dashboard for Everything

Every piece of content — events, news, alerts, the voter guide, pages, people — is managed from a single dashboard at alleghenydems.com/studio. No WordPress logins, no plugins to update, no broken page builders. Fill in a form, click Publish, and the change is live within minutes. Full CMS guide →

🗳️ Searchable Voter Guide

Residents look up every race on their ballot — from U.S. Senate to school board — with candidates, party, ballot status, and endorsements in one place. They type their street address and the guide finds their exact congressional, PA Senate, and PA House districts; a ZIP or borough name works too. How to manage it →

📋 Legislative Tracker & Scorecards

Visitors follow how our representatives in Harrisburg and Washington actually voted — organized by official, framed by their record: what each one delivered, blocked, or pushed through that's harmful, every entry linked to its source. An automated importer pulls new roll-call votes weekly; editors just review and publish. Every official also gets a shareable scorecard summarizing their record. How to manage it →

🔔 Action Alerts

A banner across the top of any page — county-wide or committee-specific — for time-sensitive calls to action: election-day reminders, volunteer pushes, endorsements. Three urgency levels set the color. How to manage it →

🏘️ Local Committee Sites

Every local committee can have its own fully branded website — its logo, colors, events, news, and a pre-filtered voter guide — connected to the county site and managed from the same dashboard. County content can be shared down to every committee site with one toggle. Committees can post their own content with a scoped login, or let the county handle it. Setting up a new committee site takes about ten minutes and no IT help. Full committee guide →

🔒 Members-Only Area

A private section at alleghenydems.com/members for committee members only. They sign in with a one-time email link — no password — and get the full member roster (with a CSV download), a documents library (bylaws, minutes, forms, training), and this guide. Access is controlled by the roster itself: only people with an active committee-member record can log in. How to manage it →

📣 Get-Out-the-Vote Tools

Two voter-facing tools turn interest into turnout: Make a Plan to Vote, a GOTV hub with a live countdown to Election Day, and Remind Me to Vote, an email sign-up whose list collects in the dashboard, ready to export for your GOTV outreach. How to manage it →
